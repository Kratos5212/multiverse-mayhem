# Lore notes behind the game rules

Research notes (mostly from the Rick and Morty Fandom wiki, through Season 9) and how the game uses them. This is an unofficial fan game.

## Getting around

| In the show | In the game |
| --- | --- |
| Rick's portal gun: green portals, limited fluid, made from rare crystals | Ricks own one. Charges recharge; Portal Fluid refills one |
| Morty borrows or steals it all the time | Morty has a borrowed gun with one charge; anyone else can borrow (steal) Rick's from the garage workbench (2 jumps), or grab a charge by knocking out a gun owner |
| Evil Morty's gold gun, the only kind that leaves the Central Finite Curve | Evil Morty's gun is gold; others need Golden Portal Fluid. Only gold portals reach worlds outside the Curve |
| Portals stay open; others can follow | Anyone can walk through an open portal |
| Citadel of Ricks: a pocket dimension anyone in the Curve can reach | Citadel Portal Station: free for Ricks and Mortys, a fee for everyone else |
| Interdimensional Customs (Federation, "Pilot") | Customs gate at the Federation: free for troopers |
| Space Cruiser crosses a universe but needs the portal gun for other dimensions | Ships: the Moon is a short hop, other planets in the home universe need the Turbo Engine, other dimensions the Dimension Drive |
| Home-universe planets: Bird World, Squanch, Gazorpazorp, Gear World, Unity's planet, Purge Planet, Blips and Chitz, the Federation | Those share the home "space" and are reachable by ship |
| Microverse in the car battery, Anatomy Park inside Ruben, Froopyland behind the chalk door | Pocket worlds, only through those doors |

## Earth

The Smiths live just outside Seattle. The game splits Earth into the Smith neighbourhood, Downtown (Merpal's Mall, Shoney's, Needful Things, Dr. Wong's office), Washington, D.C. (the White House and its Kennedy tunnels, President Andre Curtis, the Pentagon) and Area 51 (the sonic stage from "Get Schwifty"). Highways link them. No official map exists, so the layouts are our own.

TVs on Earth are normal. Rick "fixed" his own TV with crystallised Xanthenite in "Rixty Minutes", so it gets interdimensional cable.

## The same Earth in other dimensions

Prime (Cronenberged), C-131 (the Replacement Dimension), the Parmesan Dimension (the current home), C-137 (Rick's origin), Cronenberg World, C-500A (Beth and Jerry never married), the Fascist, Shrimp and Teddy universes, and J19ζ7 (Doofus Rick). The game has Prime, Fascist, Shrimp, Teddy and C-500A, each on the Smith street plan.

## More of the multiverse

Snake Planet and its time-travelling Snake Terminators ("Rattlestar Ricklactica"), Giant World up the beanstalk ("Meeseeks and Destroy"), Mr. Nimbus's Atlantis (S5E1), the dream world and Scary Terry ("Lawnmower Dog"), Doofus Rick's J19ζ7, and two other times: the split timeline from "A Rickle in Time" and Earth before the asteroid, with the dinosaurs who come back in "JuRicksic Mort".

## Time

Shleemypants, a fourth-dimensional Time Cop ("A Rickle in Time", "Rattlestar Ricklactica", S9E1), arrests third-dimensional beings who mess with time. In S9E1 Evil Morty froze time and was taken to Time Prison. In the game, using a Time Crystal brings Shleemypants after you.

## Temperaments (bot and local behaviour)

- **Gromflomite troopers:** hunt Ricks, bird people and Squanchies.
- **Evil Morty:** hunts Ricks.
- **Cronenbergs and Pickle Rick:** pick fights.
- **Jerry, Beanlings and Unity:** never start one.
- **Guards:** police, the Secret Service and Marines protect people around them.
