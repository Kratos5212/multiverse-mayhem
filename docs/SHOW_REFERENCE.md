# Show reference notes (for modelling)

Collected from the Rick and Morty fan wiki (rickandmorty.fandom.com) and articles. Colours are estimates.

## Rick's garage
- An ordinary suburban garage: exposed wooden roof beams, khaki/olive-beige drywall walls (~#B9B08A), darker wood studs (~#7A5A3A), grey concrete floor (~#8E8E88) with oil stains. Hanging shop light / bare bulbs over the workbench; daylight from the sectional roll-up door with overhead tracks.
- Door to the house (opens into the green-walled kitchen) beside a curio shelf: skull, jar of eyeballs, old camcorder, crown, goblin totem, mace, a box labelled "TIME TRAVEL STUFF".
- Rick's big U-shaped workbench with cabinets: gadgets, a CRT monitor, wires, beakers, a vise, a desk lamp; dead flies on the counter. A wall clock (always 2:00) above a calendar; a bulletin board.
- Washing machine and dryer (white) against a side wall; turning the washer knob opens a gun stash.
- The Space Cruiser parks nose-out by the garage door: a grey-silver saucer/car with a bubble windshield, two seats, engine pods both sides. The microverse battery lives in it.
- A secret hatch in the corner opposite the door leads down to the labs.
- Exterior: two-storey suburban house near Seattle, pale tan siding, white trim, dark brown-grey roof, off-white roll-up garage door, concrete driveway (cracked since S1E11), white backyard shed with brown roof.

## Citadel of Ricks
- Exterior: a huge station in an orange-brown nebula: one big central glass dome with three smaller domed districts on arms (one is Mortyburg), plates and spires hanging underneath.
- Interior: a mega-city of clean white/pale-grey curved buildings, teal accents, glass transit tubes, fountains and trees; a giant Rick statue on a central column; the Council of Ricks chamber; Mortytown (grungier, neon: Morty Mart, The Creepy Morty club, Bail Bonds, News Stand); billboards ("Simple Rick's Wafers").

## Other places
- Smith town: generic Pacific-Northwest suburb, curving streets, pastel houses, lawns, sidewalks, evergreens. Harry Herpson High: low flat-roofed beige/brick building with a sign.
- Blips and Chitz: dark arcade in space with a ceiling window of stars; neon cabinets; the Roy VR chair.
- Gazorpazorp: barren orange-red surface for the males; females live in a pastel, columned underground city.
- Bird World: watery, rocky, lush; wooden structures built into trees; cities with trees growing out of buildings.
- Planet Squanch: red grass; houses like cat climbing towers (platforms, ladders, toys on strings); blood-sucking plants.
- Froopyland: always sunny, bouncy ground, honey swamps, a rainbow river with a waterfall, a fairy-tale castle, pastel creatures.
- Anatomy Park: organ-themed rides inside Ruben (Pirates of the Pancreas, the Bone Train); fleshy pinks and reds, veins.
- Purge Planet: pastoral, old-fashioned (Star Trek "Return of the Archons"), robes, a cliff-side lighthouse.
- Dog world: dogs in egg-shaped, two-legged walker mechs; humans as pets.
- Unity's planet: low gravity, green and blue grass, purple bushes, lavender mountains, trees with fluid bladders.
- Federation prison: barbed-wire compound on a rocky planet (S2), metal station (S3); grey-blue corridors.
- Gear World: all-metal mechanical surface; Geartown with cog-shaped buildings in brass and steel, coloured transparent parts.
- Microverse: Earth-like slightly retro suburbs; people stomp gooble boxes to make power.
- Cronenberg World: Earth's suburbs overrun by pink flesh monsters; the Smith house still stands.
- Post-apocalyptic Earth: sand desert, Death Stalker camp of tents and spiked car wrecks around the Blood Dome arena.
- Pizza / Phone / Furniture universes: otherwise normal Earth streets, with roles swapped.
- Beyond the Central Finite Curve: the Curve is a helix of glowing golden orbs; beyond its wall, an open void of countless universes (golden portals).
- Interdimensional Cable: Real Fake Doors showroom, Ants in my Eyes Johnson's electronics store, Hamster-in-Butt World (hamsters living in humans), Two Brothers, the Plumbus factory.

## Characters (height relative to Morty)
- Rick ~1.45: lanky, spiky grey-blue hair, unibrow, lab coat over turquoise shirt, brown pants.
- Morty 1.0: round head, brown hair, yellow tee, blue pants.
- Summer ~1.15: ginger ponytail, pink tank top, white capris. Beth ~1.3: blonde bob, red top. Jerry ~1.35: green polo.
- Birdperson: tall, eagle wings, green feathery helmet with white dots, red skirt, yellow gloves and boots.
- Mr. Poopybutthole ~0.6: yellow, top hat, white gloves. Squanchy: short orange cat-person, blue nose, goatee.
- Meeseeks: tall powder blue, big head. Unity hosts (Nippalians): blue skin, three yellow-tipped head protrusions.
- Gazorpian males: big, red, four arms plus two small head arms, four eyes, fangs. Gromflomites: insect people, dull green, conical torso, big red compound eyes, pincers.
- Gear people: transparent coloured torsos with spinning gears, gears for ears.
